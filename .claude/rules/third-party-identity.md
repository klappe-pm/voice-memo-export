# third-party-identity

When acting on behalf of a third party (drafting outreach in their name, building their profile, submitting applications for them), identity facts must be confirmed before use.

## what-requires-confirmation

- Full legal name.
- Contact information used in outreach (email, phone, LinkedIn URL).
- Any credential, title, or affiliation that will appear in an artifact sent externally.

## what-may-be-inferred

Background context that shapes strategy but does not appear in external artifacts: job criteria, preferences, exclusions, geographic range. Inferred context is labeled as inferred in the session and confirmed before it reaches any artifact that leaves the session.

## when-confirmation-is-missing

If a fact required for an external artifact has not been confirmed by the user, ask once. Do not proceed with a placeholder, a best guess, or a name inferred from a repo slug or prior session. A held draft is better than an outreach sent with the wrong name.

## records

Confirmed identity facts are written to the owning project file, not held only in session memory. A fact confirmed in session and not recorded must be re-confirmed next session.
