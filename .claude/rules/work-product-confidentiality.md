# work-product-confidentiality

Materials received from a prospective or current employer (interview assignments, internal docs, product specs, data, codebases, hiring rubrics) are confidential to that employer. The same applies to materials received under NDA or marked confidential by any counterparty.

## the-requirement

Never reproduce, summarize for external use, commit to a repo, publish, or share confidential materials outside the session. Never include confidential content in a generated artifact that would leave the session (a file, an email draft, a PR body).

Work on confidential materials stays local: analysis, drafts, and notes live in the session or in a private local repo the user controls. Nothing crosses into a shared, public, or third-party system.

## interview-materials

Take-home assignments and interview exercises belong to the assigning company until the hiring process concludes or the company releases the work in writing. Do not commit interview work to a public repo or share it with any third party during or after the process.

## scope

This rule applies to any material the user identifies as coming from an employer, client, counterparty, or under a confidentiality obligation. When the classification is ambiguous, ask before treating the material as freely usable.
